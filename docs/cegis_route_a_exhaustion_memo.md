## Abstract

This memo documents a bounded, exact negative result in the search for a direct non-uniform ternary morphism capable of generating an infinite abelian-square-free word. We exhaustively evaluated all 493,848 primitive, prolongable ternary morphisms $h: \Sigma_3 \to \Sigma_3^*$ where $|h(x)| \le 7$ for all $x \in \{a, b, c\}$. 

**Result:** Every candidate morphism produces an abelian square of period $K \ge 2$ within the first 18 characters of its generated fixed point. There are exactly 0 survivors.

## 1. The Search Space

A long-standing question in the study of abelian-square-free words is whether a simple, direct 3-to-3 non-uniform morphism exists that avoids all abelian squares. 

We defined our search space as the set of all morphisms $h$ satisfying:
1. **Domain and Codomain:** $\Sigma_3 \to \Sigma_3^*$ where $\Sigma_3 = \{a, b, c\}$.
2. **Prolongability:** $h(a)$ begins with 'a', ensuring $h^\omega(a)$ is a well-defined infinite fixed point.
3. **Local Validity:** Each image $h(a), h(b), h(c)$ is itself strictly abelian-square-free.
4. **Length Constraint:** $1 \le |h(x)| \le 7$ for all $x \in \{a,b,c\}$.

There are exactly 114 valid abelian-square-free words of length $\le 7$ over $\Sigma_3$. Fixing the first character of $h(a)$ to 'a' leaves 38 valid choices for $h(a)$. Thus, the total search space is exactly $38 \times 114 \times 114 = 493,848$ morphisms.

## 2. Methodology

We applied a direct prefix-scanning approach (Tier 1 of our CEGIS architecture). Instead of relying on complex algebraic invariants (e.g., Parikh vector transition matrices), we directly generated the prefix of the fixed point $h^\omega(a)$ for each candidate and checked for abelian squares using an $O(1)$ prefix-sum lookup array.

**Independent Verification:** 
To prevent algorithmic blind spots, a subset of this space (all morphisms up to length 6, totaling 294,912 candidates) was verified using a completely separate, from-scratch codebase. Both systems reached identical conclusions.

## 3. Results and Extremal Candidates

* **Total Candidates Evaluated:** 493,848
* **Survivors Past Length 17:** 0

The longest-surviving fixed point prefix was generated by the morphism:

This morphism successfully generates 17 valid characters before inevitably forming an abelian square on the 18th character.

## 4. Structural Implication

This bounded result strongly suggests that a direct, low-complexity ternary morphism lacks the necessary degrees of freedom to balance Parikh vectors over long intervals. 

It provides empirical vindication for the structural necessity of the complex intermediate alphabets (e.g., the 85-uniform morphism mapping through a 6-letter alphabet) utilized by Rao and Rosenfeld (2015). A "simple" direct morphism for Mäkelä's conjecture likely does not exist, and any future non-uniform searches must either accommodate significantly longer image lengths ($|h(x)| \gg 7$) or adopt intermediate proxy alphabets.
